How do I find my Satellite Radio ID?
On most satellite radio devices, tune to Channel 0 (Zero) to view the 8-character Radio ID / ESN. Alternatively, check the physical label on the back/bottom of your tuner dock.
What should I do if my radio shows "Check Antenna"?
This warning usually indicates a loose connection or damaged cable between your radio dock and the external antenna. Make sure the antenna has a clear line of sight to the sky without obstruction.
