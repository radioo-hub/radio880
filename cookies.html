Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 2/14/2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites recognize you and enhance your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use our website
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to show you relevant advertisements

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by third-party services like analytics tools

4. Cookie Consent

🍪 When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

5. Controlling Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, blocking cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Third-Party Cookie Policies

Third parties may use cookies on our site. You can opt out of analytics cookies by visiting the relevant opt-out pages.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy at any time. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
