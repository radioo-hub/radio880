How to Find Your Radio ID / ESN

A step-by-step guide to locating your unique 8-character satellite Radio ID or Electronic Serial Number (ESN) on any radio receiver.

What is a Radio ID / ESN?

Your Radio ID (also referred to as an ESN or RID) is a unique 8-character alphanumeric code assigned to your satellite radio receiver. It contains numbers and letters (excluding I, O, S, and F). You need this code whenever you want to activate service, refresh your signal, or manage account preferences.

3 Quick Ways to Locate Your Radio ID

Method 1: Tune to Channel 0

The fastest and easiest way for most satellite radio tuners and vehicle receivers.

  • Turn on your radio unit.
  • Use the scroll knob or dial to select Channel 0 (Zero).
  • Your 8-character Radio ID will display directly on the screen.

Method 2: Check Physical Unit Label

Ideal for portable plug-and-play docks, home receivers, and standalone tuners.

  • Power off and unmount the radio receiver from its dock.
  • Look at the printed sticker on the back or bottom of the device.
  • Locate the 8-character code next to "Radio ID" or "ESN".

Method 3: System Menu Settings

For factory-installed in-dash car radios and touchscreen infotainment systems.

  • Go to Menu / Settings on your stereo screen.
  • Select System Info or Radio Setup.
  • Scroll down to find your 8-digit Radio ID displayed.
