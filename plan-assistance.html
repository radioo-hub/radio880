Independent Plan Selection Guidance

Choosing the right radio package depends on what you like to listen to and where you listen. Whether you want basic music channels for your car commute or full access including live sports play-by-play, news, and phone app streaming, our specialists can guide you through the available features.

Popular Package Overview Guidance

Music & Entertainment Tier

Ideal for drivers who want commercial-free music, talk shows, and news entertainment while on the road.

  • Ad-free music channels
  • News, comedy & talk radio
  • In-car satellite reception
  • Standard channel selection

Platinum / Sports Tier

Designed for sports enthusiasts and power listeners who want maximum coverage including live games.

  • All Music & Entertainment channels
  • Live NFL, MLB, NBA, NHL play-by-play
  • Howard Stern & premium talk channels
  • Mobile app streaming included

Streaming Only Package

Perfect for home receivers, smart speakers, computers, and smartphone listening without a car radio.

  • No satellite dish/antenna needed
  • Smartphone & Tablet App access
  • Smart speaker integration (Alexa/Google)
  • Custom ad-free music stations
