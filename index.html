Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Sirius XM activation and service

What is Sirius XM?▼
Sirius XM is a satellite radio service offering hundreds of channels of music, sports, news, talk, and entertainment across North America.
How do I activate my service?▼
Call us at +1 (406) 747-4507 with your Radio ID. Our experts will guide you through the quick activation process.
What equipment do I need?▼
You need a Sirius XM-compatible receiver or in-car radio system, a power source, and an antenna with clear reception.

Yes, Sirius XM requires a subscription. We can help you choose the best plan for your listening needs.
Can I activate online?▼
Yes, but for personalized assistance and troubleshooting, we recommend calling our experts at +1 (406) 747-4507.
What areas are covered?▼
Sirius XM covers the entire continental US, Canada, and parts of Mexico. Coverage may vary in remote areas.
